WHAT THE DEN IS
---------------
FastAPI + SQLAlchemy/Alembic (SQLite) + Jinja2 + APScheduler media manager with a built-in
libtorrent client, Discover (TMDB), Plex login + library scan, Requests with quotas, and the
HoltOS Glass UI. Companion apps: KDE client (the-den-client) and Android app (the-den-android).
